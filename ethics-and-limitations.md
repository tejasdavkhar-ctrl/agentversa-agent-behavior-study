# Ethics and Limitations

## Simulation and Scope
AgentVersa is a simulation and does not perform real legal work, professional judgment, medical decision-making, or other real-world operational tasks. Any conclusions drawn from these scenarios are limited to the simulated environment and should not be treated as professional advice or an operational safety assessment.

## Variability of LLM Output
Large language model outputs can vary between runs, even when prompts are similar. Differences in wording, context, model version, sampling settings, and system behavior can affect what an agent says or does.

## Factors That Influence Behavior
Behavior in these scenarios may be influenced by:
- prompt wording and scenario design;
- model limitations and reasoning constraints;
- context memory and role framing;
- relationships among agents;
- platform design and timing constraints;
- assumptions embedded in the environment or instructions.

## Risk Scores and Assessments
Any risk scores or risk assessments used in this study are simulated evaluations unless separately calibrated against a real-world standard, dataset, or validated rubric.

## Limits of Generalization
A small number of scenarios cannot establish production safety, professional competence, or broad generalization to real-world tasks. Findings may be suggestive but are not definitive.

## Confidentiality and Privacy
No confidential, personally identifiable, or sensitive information should be published in this repository or in related notes. Research should avoid disclosing names, private details, or proprietary materials.

---

This portfolio is intended for educational and research use only. It should be read with caution and interpreted within the limits of a simulation.
